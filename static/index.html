Model card

PR-AUC, 5-fold cross-validation with whole races held out. Tyre age alone barely beats guessing. Most of the signal is recent pace, so the model mostly notices that a driver is already slowing down.

The cliff is a definition: pace at least 1.0 s off the stint best, for 2 laps in a row. A different rule would give different labels.
